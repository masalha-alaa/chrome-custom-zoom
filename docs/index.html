Free · Open source · No account required

01 / EXACT ZOOM

Find your perfect size

Type any whole-number percentage from 25% to 500%. Chrome's native zoom adjusts the page.

02 / YOUR OWN STEP

Adjust at your pace

Choose how many percentage points each − or + click changes. Start with 5, or set your own step from 1 to 475.

03 / FIVE FAVORITES

Save it with a star

Keep up to five presets, sorted in ascending order. Apply one instantly or unstar it to remove it from your saved list.

Your preferences stay on your device.

No analytics, ads, or account. The extension stores only your step size and presets locally. Chrome handles website zoom settings.

Read the privacy policy →

Simple from the first click.

Pin the extension, open its popup, and enter a percentage. Press Enter to apply it. Use Reset to restore Chrome's configured default zoom. Some internal or restricted pages do not allow extension-controlled zoom.
